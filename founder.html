Founder profile

An education career focused on purposeful learning.

Itoro-Obong Akie is a Learning Solutions Specialist, Educational Consultant, and Early Years Professional with extensive experience designing and delivering impactful learning experiences for children, young people, educators, and communities.

She brings expertise gained through leadership, teaching, consultancy, curriculum development, training, and educational programme design across a range of educational settings.

Her work focuses on educational consultancy, learning design, curriculum development, literacy, SEND provision, staff training, learner support, and community learning.

Through AJIS Learning Solutions, she works to inspire confidence, unlock potential, and create meaningful learning experiences that lead to lasting success.

A leadership style shaped by compassion, vision, and growth.
