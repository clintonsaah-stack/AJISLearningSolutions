Privacy Policy

Information about the current AJIS Learning Solutions website preview.

Enquiry Forms and FormSubmit

When you submit a valid consultation, contact or tuition form, your name, contact details, enquiry category, programme of interest, message, submission date and privacy acknowledgement are sent from your browser to FormSubmit for forwarding to AJIS at ajis.tuition@gmail.com. FormSubmit is a third-party service and processes submissions under its own terms and privacy policy. AJIS must complete FormSubmit’s one-time recipient activation before enquiry emails can reach the inbox. The website itself does not store form entries. Avoid including sensitive, medical, or unnecessary information.

Direct Contact and Social Links

Telephone and email links open the relevant application on your device; this website does not place calls or send email through those links. The separate WhatsApp button is optional and opens only when you choose it. Social media links open third-party services. Those providers may receive technical information when you visit them and handle your data under their own privacy policies. The enquiry email address displayed on this site is ajis.tuition@gmail.com.

FAQ Assistant

The quick-answer assistant uses a small set of factual responses based on information published on this website. It does not use an AI service, send questions to a server, or retain conversation history. Questions and answers remain in the current page until you leave or reload it.

Website Requests and Hosting

The site is designed for static hosting. A hosting provider may process ordinary technical request data. The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts; the provider may receive standard connection information when your browser retrieves them. Website photographs and the homepage video and poster are stored with this website.

Policy Status

This notice describes the current website features and is not legal advice. Before launch, the AJIS website owner should confirm FormSubmit activation, hosting and contact arrangements, any future data processing or retention, and arrange appropriate legal review.
