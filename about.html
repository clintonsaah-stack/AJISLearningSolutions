Our story

Education shaped by purpose, possibility, and people.

AJIS Learning Solutions is an educational consultancy supporting learners, educators, parents, schools, organisations, and communities. Its work brings together educational expertise and practical support to create meaningful learning experiences.

The consultancy focuses on educational guidance, curriculum and learning design, early years education, literacy, inclusive practice, staff development, and learner support.
