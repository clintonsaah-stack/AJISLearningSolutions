Meet Our Founder

Learning That Unlocks Potential.

Itoro-Obong Akie is a Learning Solutions Specialist, Educational Consultant and Early Years Professional. She has extensive experience designing and delivering impactful learning experiences for children, young people, educators and communities.

Her work is grounded in a commitment to meaningful learning, stronger educational practice and the potential of every learner.

The Vision Behind AJIS

A Learning Environment Where Everyone Can Grow.

Itoro-Obong established AJIS Learning Solutions to bring her experience, professional insight and commitment to education into a dedicated consultancy supporting learners, parents, educators, schools, organisations and communities.

Her vision is to contribute to a learning environment where every individual can develop confidence, build capability and achieve meaningful progress.
